How it works

  1. Select blood group & units needed
  2. We match the nearest verified blood bank with stock
  3. Estimated costs breakdown:
    Blood processing: ₹1,500–2,000/unit
    Sample testing: ₹200–500
    Medical transport: ₹150–300
    Coordination fee: ₹250
  4. Your hospital coordinates with the blood bank via medical courier for sampling & testing
  5. Courier picks up tested blood and delivers to your hospital
  6. Track every step in "My Orders" – from confirmation to delivery
